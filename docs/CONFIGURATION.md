# WIIIUI Forever

A Lua recreation of Fiur’s [WIIIUI](https://github.com/Fiurs-Hearth/WIIIUI) for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 (interface 16001). It uses the original Warcraft III console textures and Forever’s own action buttons, minimap, chat, unit frames, and menus. It has no required addons.

Copy the `WIIIUIForever` folder to `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` and enable **WIIIUI Forever** on the character screen.

- The Human theme loads by default. Use the cog above chat or `/wiiiui human`, `/wiiiui orc`, `/wiiiui undead`, or `/wiiiui nightelf` to switch.
- The console shows slots for the action bars you enable in WoW’s Action Bars settings. Enable bars 2 and 3 to fill the upper two rows. The console fits the standard horizontal, 12-button layout.
- Use the cog’s **Character panel** submenu to choose a stat for each of the four slots under the level bar. Choices are saved per character. The default stats are weapon damage, armor reduction, spell damage, and bonus healing. Other choices include equipped ammo, main-hand and ranged weapon DPS, last fight average DPS from WoW’s damage meter, and melee critical chance.
- **Combat DPS (resets each fight)** shows your current combat session’s DPS. **Highest damage ability (DPS)** shows the native meter’s leading damage ability, its icon, and its DPS contribution; hover for the ability tooltip. Both clear at the start of the next fight and retain the completed result between fights. These choices use Forever’s native damage meter when it is available.
- Armor uses your equipped chest item’s icon; an empty chest slot uses the default armor icon.
- The portrait area uses the native player frame for clicking and opening its unit menu.
- Use **Cog → Health and resource text → Health / Resource** to choose **Numbers**, **Percentage**, **Both**, or **None** separately for the two portrait bars. Numbers show current/maximum; Both adds the percentage. Resource follows your current mana, rage, energy, or other power type. Choices update immediately and save per character; the default is None.
- A blue segment on the level bar shows rested XP ahead of your current XP. Hover over the level bar to fade from your level and class to current XP / required XP. The tooltip shows the percentage and rested amount. The cog’s **Character panel** menu also offers **Rested XP** as a stat, shown as a percentage of the current level.
- The six quick slots between the action bars and chat accept items and spells dragged from your bags, spellbook, or action bars. Left-click to use a slot and right-click to clear it. Use the cog's **Quick slots** checkbox to show or hide them for each character; hidden slots keep their assignments and the original decorative panel returns. Visibility changes made in combat apply when combat ends. Change assignments outside combat.
- Use the cog’s **Portrait framing** option to adjust the animated model on the X, Y, and Z axes. The HUD updates live and each character saves its own offsets. **Reset portrait** in that window, or **Reset portrait to default** in the cog, sets all three axes to zero and restores the default camera framing.
- **Lock portrait framing** in the cog is on by default. After the idle model loads, the addon rebuilds the native head camera and lets it settle before capturing its position, target, and roll as a fixed custom camera. That anchor saves per character and model and is restored after attacks, appearance refreshes, level changes, and reloads. Version 0.6.4 recaptures older camera anchors once, retaining X/Y/Z; there are no hard-coded race offsets. Switch the lock off to return to the automatic camera. **Reapply saved** restores the captured view without clearing the offsets; **Reset portrait** clears both the offsets and the captured camera so it can be framed afresh.
- Press **C** to open the Forsaken-styled Character window. Drag the Character title or the inventory heading outside combat. The inventory follows the Character window until you move it separately. Each character saves its own positions.
- Select **View** on the Character inventory panel, or **Character inventory** in the cog menu, to show all carried bags, the backpack, a specific equipped bag, or hide the panel. **Open bags** opens WoW’s bag window.
- **Cog → PvP and combat indicators** offers independent **Icon and text**, **Icon only**, **Text only**, or **Hidden** modes for the two status badges, plus **Warcraft frame** / **Simple frame** styles. You can hide the PvP badge while unflagged and hide the combat badge while idle. Hover icons for their full status. These choices save per character and apply immediately.
- The optional Warcraft III-style **Session ribbon** at the top tracks XP/hour, gold received, gold/hour, session time, and estimated time to level. Click **Session** or use **Cog → Session ribbon** to show/hide it, change or hide any of its five fields (including total XP, current money, and rested XP), or **Reset session statistics**. Totals survive `/reload`; a fresh login starts a new session. Gold received counts positive money changes without subtracting spending.
- **Session ribbon → Backing** chooses a faction-themed **Warcraft III frame** with alpha-shaped edges or a **Transparent** backing. **Show day/night clock** adds the original Warcraft III sun/moon dial between Session and the fields. It advances with realm time, uses Forever’s reported day/night state, and shows the game clock on hover. Both choices save per character.
- Main-hand DPS and melee crit use the equipped main-hand weapon icon. Ranged DPS uses the equipped ranged weapon icon.
- On login, WIIIUI creates or selects the account-wide **WIIIUI Forever Bars** layout in Edit Mode. An older character layout can seed the shared layout on first use. Select the account layout in Edit Mode, move native elements such as the objective tracker or swing timer, and click **Save**. Your other characters use that saved layout. `/wiiiui off` restores the previous layout for the current character.
- To report a bug, open **Settings → AddOns → WIIIUI Forever** and click the bug icon in the upper-right corner. Copy the selected link into your browser to open a new GitHub issue.
- Minimap buttons provided by LibDBIcon appear in centered rows above the zone name and square map.
- Use `/wiiiui off` to restore the native layout and `/wiiiui on` to bring the console back. The console also steps aside in Edit Mode and the native Gamepad UI.

The console textures come from [Fiur’s WIIIUI](https://github.com/Fiurs-Hearth/WIIIUI). The Forsaken panel textures, faction cursor artwork, and the ribbon/status console frames, ornaments, resource icons, and faction flags come from the locally installed Warcraft III: Reforged archive. The ribbon and Warcraft-frame status badges follow the selected console theme. The cursor artwork is included as reference; the WoW Forever client supplies the pointing hand. [`tools/convert-wc3-hud-assets.py`](../tools/convert-wc3-hud-assets.py) converts the extracted DDS artwork to uncompressed RGBA TGA without resampling or recoloring it.
